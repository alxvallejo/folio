# Slow software

Most apps want your attention. The ones I keep coming back to ask for very little: they open fast, they stay out of the way, and they never rearrange themselves while I'm reading.

## What slow means

Slow doesn't mean sluggish. It means the software moves at the speed of the *person* using it:

- It remembers where you were.
- It saves quietly and never asks.
- It changes only when you change it.

## Why I write here

I keep my drafts as plain Markdown files, which means any tool can open them years from now. The [Folio](https://example.com) editor just gets out of the way while I type.
